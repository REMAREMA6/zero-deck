# CIRCLES Method™: What Each Step Actually Does

**Source:** Lewis C. Lin (author of *Decode and Conquer*) — official blog
**URL:** https://www.lewis-lin.com/blog/circles-method-what-each-step-actually-does
**Fetched:** 2026-09-30

Many of you know the CIRCLES from interview prep or Decode and Conquer. But here's the truth: most people know what it is and how to use it — very few know why it works. That's why I built this quick cheat sheet: to help you internalize not just the steps, but the purpose behind them.

## Breaking Down the Steps

**C** = Clarify the Goal and Boundaries 🧭 → Prevents solving the wrong problem.

**I** = Identify Customers + Stakeholders 👥 → Ensures you don't miss critical stakeholders.

**R** = Report Needs 📋 → Avoids generic pain points.

**C** = Cut Through Prioritization ✂️ → Stops you from trying to solve everything.

**L** = List Solutions 💡 → Prevents obvious or boring answers.

**E** = Evaluate Options ⚖️ → Choose wisely by weighing what matters.

**S** = Summarize Your Recommendation 🎯 → Avoids a rambling conclusion.
