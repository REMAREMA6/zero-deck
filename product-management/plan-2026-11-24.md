## The seven steps, mapped to this work

1. **Comprehend** — fix the goal (what the MVP must achieve) and the success metric it implies;
   state constraints (licensing, solo build, no lab) and context; every assumption named.
2. **Identify** — one cohort defined by context and friction (a real person hitting a suspicious
   file/link on their phone), plus the second side of the market (the admin/analyst), named and
   acknowledged.
3. **Report** — the needs behind the feature list: needs stated as jobs (trust a verdict, contain a
   threat, learn what happened), user stories with outcome clauses; inferred needs marked inferred.
4. **Cut** — rank the needs on named axes (frequency of the pain, acuteness, alignment with the
   goal); choose; state out loud what is being given up — the cuts land in "later release".
5. **List** — for the chosen need, two or three genuinely different designs spanning quick win /
   core bet / horizon (e.g., different verdict-presentation or quarantine-interaction shapes, not
   variations of one screen).
6. **Evaluate** — axes fixed before reading options; drawbacks of the favoured option written down;
   weight on viable and lovable, not buildable; who absorbs a wrong verdict.
7. **Summarize** — one recommendation, one testable metric (who, what behaviour, what period,
   compared to what), the trade-off named, link back to the goal.
